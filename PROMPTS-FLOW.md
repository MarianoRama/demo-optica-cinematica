# Prompts para Google Flow — Óptica Lúmina

Usá las dos imágenes que compartiste como referencias visuales. Buscamos una pieza de producto con luz suave, fondo blanco grisáceo y espacio limpio para el texto a la izquierda. El texto se agrega en la web, nunca en el video.

Conviene generar un clip por modelo y hacer la transición en la web. Un cambio directo de material o forma dentro del mismo clip puede deformar los puentes, patillas o lentes. Los resultados deben revisarse; ningún prompt asegura geometría perfecta.

## 1. Clip horizontal — metal

Create an 8-second premium eyewear product film based on the supplied reference image of the gray metal eyeglasses. Landscape 16:9. Preserve the exact frame shape, bridge, hinges, temples and transparent lenses from the reference. The eyeglasses float freely in an empty seamless studio, with no pedestal or supporting object, positioned entirely in the right 55% of the frame. Keep the left 45% empty with a soft, almost white neutral-gray background, reserved for website typography. Locked camera, no zoom or pan. A very slow, delicate floating motion and a tiny three-quarter rotation of no more than 5 degrees, returning to the starting pose by the final frame for a seamless loop. Large softbox lighting, restrained realistic reflections, soft contact shadow, refined editorial product photography, physically coherent geometry. Keep the full eyeglasses visible with generous margins. No text, letters, logos, watermark, people, hands, additional objects, plinth, pedestal, platform, box, cube, stand, exaggerated particles, dramatic colors, lens distortion, warped temples or duplicated frames. Silent visual asset.

## 2. Clip horizontal — acetato marrón

Create an 8-second premium eyewear product film based on the supplied reference image of the brown rounded eyeglasses. Landscape 16:9. Preserve the exact rounded frame silhouette, warm brown texture, bridge, hinges, temples and transparent lenses. Match the metal-eyeglasses clip in camera position, product scale, light direction, background color and empty seamless studio setting. Position the complete eyeglasses in the right 55% of the frame. Keep the left 45% empty and softly lit for website text. Locked camera, no zoom or pan. A slow, barely perceptible floating motion with a tiny three-quarter rotation of no more than 5 degrees; return to the initial pose at the end for a seamless loop. Soft, premium studio lighting, realistic highlights, understated shadow. The frame must remain rigid and geometrically consistent throughout. No transformation into another model, no text, logos, watermark, people, hands, extra objects, plinth, pedestal, platform, box, cube, stand, particles, wobbling, melting, duplicated lenses or warped temples. Silent visual asset.

## 3. Versión para celular

Create an 8-second vertical 9:16 premium eyewear product film using the supplied eyeglasses reference. Preserve the exact product geometry and material. Place the whole eyeglasses in the lower half of the image, centered horizontally and with generous side margins. Keep the upper 45% completely empty in a soft white-gray studio background for a headline and navigation. Match the horizontal film's lighting and restrained styling. Locked camera. Extremely subtle floating motion with a tiny rotation and an ending pose that matches the opening pose. Only a delicate diffuse shadow beneath the glasses. No plinth, pedestal, platform, box, cube, stand or supporting object. No text, logos, people, hands, extra objects, extreme motion, cropping of temples, deformation, duplicate frames or change of model. Silent visual asset.

Repetí el prompt vertical con cada referencia si querés ambos modelos en móvil.

## Qué mandar para integrarlo

- Los clips originales descargados (MP4 o WebM), sin texto agregado.
- Horizontal con los lentes a la derecha; vertical con el producto abajo.
- Si una toma te gusta más aunque no cierre como loop, también sirve: podemos hacer un fundido entre clips.

No hace falta que generes todo: con los dos clips horizontales ya podemos reemplazar la animación de imágenes; la versión móvil puede conservar los PNG hasta tener su video.
